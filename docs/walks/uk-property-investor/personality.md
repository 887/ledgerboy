# Persona — Priya, a small property investor (UK)

> A `docs/walks/` scenario persona. Fictional person, fictional assets,
> fictional valuations. Money is private — nothing here is real.

## Snapshot

- **Handle:** Priya (fictional)
- **Country / locale:** United Kingdom, `en-GB`. Currency GBP.
- **Tech comfort:** Moderate. Keeps a spreadsheet of what she owns and
  what it's worth; wants something that tracks value over time without a
  cloud account.
- **Device:** An Android phone (Android 16 / API 36).
- **Asset shape (fictional):** one buy-to-let flat, one car. Each bought
  at a known price; she revalues them by hand a couple of times a year.
  Round demo numbers (e.g. flat acquired £180,000, car £14,000).

## What Priya wants

- An **asset registry** beyond cash — real estate + a vehicle — each with
  an **acquisition cost** and a **history of valuations** she can add to.
- To see the **current value** roll up from the latest valuation, and to
  look back at how a value changed.

## What would make her bounce

- An app that only does bank transactions and has nowhere to put a flat.
- Losing the valuation history when she records a new value (she wants
  the trail, not an overwrite).
