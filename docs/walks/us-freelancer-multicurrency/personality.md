# Persona — Theo, a multi-currency freelancer (US)

> A `docs/walks/` scenario persona. Fictional person, fictional accounts,
> fictional numbers. Money is private — nothing here is real.

## Snapshot

- **Handle:** Theo (fictional)
- **Country / locale:** United States, `en-US`. Base currency he thinks
  in is USD, but he invoices European clients and holds a EUR account.
- **Tech comfort:** High. Comfortable with CSVs, exports, and the idea of
  a local-first app that talks to no server.
- **Device:** A recent Android phone (Android 16 / API 36), usually online.
- **Money shape (fictional):** a USD checking account and a EUR account.
  EUR income from clients, USD spending. Round demo numbers.

## What Theo wants

- To see **both accounts in one place** and understand his exposure
  across USD and EUR.
- To turn on **exchange rates from a real source** (the ECB, directly —
  no third-party data broker) so amounts can eventually roll up.
- **Cashflow charts** to see income vs expense month over month.

## What would make him bounce

- A pricing-service / aggregator middleman for FX. (ledgerboy uses the
  ECB directly; he likes that.)
- The app silently summing EUR + USD into one wrong number. He'd rather
  see per-currency totals than a fabricated blend.
